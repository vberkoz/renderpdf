Billing terms & currency

All plans are priced and billed in $ USD on a recurring monthly or annual basis (save 20% with annual plans: $190/yr Starter, $490/yr Professional). You can manage payment methods, download invoices, or cancel your subscription at any time via Manage billing.

Quota exhaustion policy

When you reach 100% of your monthly PDF quota, further render requests pause with an HTTP 429 Too Many Requests code to prevent unexpected costs. You can add capacity at any time by buying an on-demand overage pack (1,000 PDFs for $5 USD) or upgrading to Professional.

Cancellation & refunds

Cancel self-serve anytime via the Paddle Customer Portal ("Manage billing"). Subscriptions stay active until the end of your billing cycle. We offer a 100% refund within 14 days if usage is under 10% of monthly quota (Refund policy).

Use keys only from a trusted server or backend. Store them in a secret manager or environment variable—never in browser code.
