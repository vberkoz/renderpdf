2. Information We Collect and Process

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. We collect only the information necessary to provide, secure, and bill for the Service:

  • Account & Identity Data: When you register and sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your verified primary email address, your display name, and a persistent unique identifier (sub claim). We never receive, store, or have access to your Google account password.
  • API Request & Rendering Data: When you send documents to our API, we process the supplied HTML, Markdown, CSS, dynamic JSON variables, URL parameters, or ZIP package archives to execute Chromium headless rendering. Rendered PDFs are stored in encrypted, private Amazon S3 buckets accessible only via signed, expiring download URLs.
  • Usage & Telemetry Data: We record timestamped event records in Amazon DynamoDB capturing request ID, HTTP method, source type, byte count, render duration, and account quota utilization. For anonymous trial requests, we compute a one-way privacy-preserving SHA-256 hash of the client IP address to enforce daily rate limits; raw IP addresses are never persisted.
  • Billing & Payment Data: All financial transactions are processed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com Market Ltd. RenderPDF does not collect, process, or store full credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account details. We receive only non-sensitive transaction tokens, subscription status, and customer identifiers required to allocate account quotas.

5. GDPR Compliance (EU & UK Data Subjects)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our processing of your personal data complies with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to provide PDF generation services, create API keys, authenticate accounts, and deliver customer support.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing necessary to monitor infrastructure health, enforce rate limits, detect and block malicious content or cyberattacks, and prevent abuse.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Maintaining transaction records and cooperating with statutory compliance obligations via our Merchant of Record.

Your Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR Articles 15 through 22, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17): Request permanent deletion of your personal data and account records.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to the processing of your data where processing is based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: Lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority (such as the ICO in the UK or the relevant EU Data Protection Authority).

To exercise any of these rights, email our Data Protection team at support@renderpdf.vberkoz.com. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) days without fee.

6. CCPA and CPRA Compliance (California Residents)

This section applies to California residents pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (CPRA).

  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: RenderPDF has never sold, and will never sell or share, your personal information or document contents with third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration, nor for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Categories of Personal Information Collected: Identifiers (email address, Google profile ID, IP hash), commercial information (transaction records and quotas), and internet activity logs (API usage metrics).
  • California Consumer Rights: You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, the right to correct inaccurate personal information, and the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Exercising Your Rights: You may submit a request by contacting support@renderpdf.vberkoz.com with the subject line "CCPA Request".

7. Technical and Organizational Security Practices

We employ multi-layered technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your documents and credentials:

  • Encryption in Transit: All HTTP communications require TLS 1.3 or TLS 1.2 with modern cryptographic cipher suites; unencrypted HTTP requests are automatically redirected to HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: All PDF documents stored in Amazon S3 and metadata in Amazon DynamoDB are encrypted using AES-256 server-side encryption.
  • Strict Isolation: Customer API keys and stored templates are isolated per customer account and verified through AWS Lambda authorizers. Documents cannot be accessed across account boundaries.
  • Expiring Signed URLs: Rendered PDFs are delivered through private Amazon S3 presigned URLs that automatically expire after 15 minutes, preventing unauthorized URL indexing or sharing.
