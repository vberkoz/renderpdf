How does RenderPDF handle custom CSS and web fonts?

RenderPDF runs a real headless Chromium engine under the hood, delivering pixel-perfect fidelity with modern web standards. You have full access to CSS Grid, Flexbox, print-specific CSS rules (@page, page-break properties, margins), and custom CSS variables.

Web fonts are supported out of the box via Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, or standard @font-face definitions. You can also embed base64 fonts directly in your CSS or upload ZIP packages with local font files.

Is my document data stored or shared?

Your documents are completely private. We never share, sell, or use your data to train AI models.

Rendered PDFs are stored in private, AES-256 encrypted AWS S3 buckets and can only be downloaded via short-lived presigned URLs that expire after 15 minutes. All generated PDFs and temporary upload packages are governed by automatic 30-day deletion lifecycle policies—once the 30-day window passes, objects are permanently removed.

What happens if I exceed my monthly plan quota?

We believe in transparent, predictable billing. Once 100% of your plan's monthly PDF allowance is reached, subsequent render requests return an HTTP 429 Too Many Requests status code. You will never receive surprise overage bills or unauthorized charges.

When you need more volume immediately, on-demand overage top-up packs are available for $5 USD per 1,000 PDFs directly from your dashboard with zero downtime, or you can upgrade to a higher tier instantly with prorated pricing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions feature 1-click self-serve cancellation.

You can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your dashboard—no support tickets, phone calls, or retention funnels required. After cancellation, you retain full access to your plan and remaining monthly PDF quota until the end of your current paid billing period.
