1. How to Cancel Your Subscription

You may cancel your RenderPDF paid subscription at any time. There are no cancellation fees, no lock-in contracts, and no need to speak with sales agents. You can complete cancellation entirely self-serve in seconds:

✓
Step-by-Step Self-Serve Cancellation

1. Open your developer dashboard at https://renderpdf.vberkoz.com/app/.
2. Navigate to the Billing section, or open /app/?view=billing.
3. Click the "Manage billing" button.
4. A secure portal tab opens to the Paddle Customer Portal.
5. Click "Cancel Subscription" under your active plan and confirm.

Upon cancellation, Paddle (our Merchant of Record) immediately sends an automated confirmation email to your registered address verifying that your recurring billing has stopped.

If you prefer assistance, you can also email us at support@renderpdf.vberkoz.com from your registered Google account email, and our support team will execute the cancellation on your behalf within 24 hours.

2. What Happens to Your Access and Quota

When you cancel a subscription:

  • Access Continues Until Period End: You continue to enjoy all benefits of your plan, including remaining monthly PDF generation quota, API keys, and stored templates, until the conclusion of your current paid billing period (whether on a monthly or annual billing cycle).
  • No Further Charges: Your card or payment method will not be charged again.
  • Automatic Free Tier Transition: Once the billing period concludes, your account automatically transitions to our Free tier (25 PDFs per month). Your existing API keys remain valid and your stored templates are preserved.
  • Reactivating Anytime: You can choose to reactivate or upgrade your subscription at any future time directly from the dashboard.

3. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Cancelling stops future renewals. If you are unsatisfied and would like a full refund for your most recent payment, you are protected by our 14-day money-back guarantee:

Customers are eligible for a 100% full refund within 14 calendar days of payment if total PDF generation usage has not exceeded 10% of their plan's monthly quota (less than 500 PDFs for Starter, or less than 2,000 PDFs for Professional, on either monthly or annual subscriptions). To request a refund, see our Refund Policy.

4. Overage Credit Packs

Purchases of on-demand overage credit packs (1,000 PDFs for $5 USD) are one-time payments and do not recur. They remain on your account until consumed. If you have not used any portion of an overage pack, it is eligible for a full refund within 14 days of purchase upon request.

5. Merchant of Record Processing

All subscription payments, customer portals, tax handling, and cancellations are conducted through our authorized Merchant of Record, Paddle.com Market Ltd (Judd House, 18-29 Mora Street, London, EC1V 8BT, UK). Paddle provides the secure self-serve billing portal accessible from your RenderPDF dashboard.
