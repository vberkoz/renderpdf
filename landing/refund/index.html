1. 14-Day Full Refund Guarantee

At RenderPDF, we want you to be completely confident in our document rendering infrastructure. If you subscribe to a paid RenderPDF plan and find that our service does not meet your technical requirements or expectations, you are eligible for a 100% full refund within fourteen (14) calendar days of your initial purchase or subscription renewal date.

✓
Fair Use Protection

To prevent fraudulent consumption, refunds are provided under our fair-use condition: your PDF rendering usage must not have exceeded 10% of your plan's monthly quota during the billing period.

2. Quota Usage Thresholds for Refunds

To qualify for a full refund within the 14-day window, your usage must remain within the 10% usage threshold for your respective plan:

If your usage has exceeded the 10% threshold, your subscription remains active until the end of your billing cycle, and automatic renewal can be cancelled immediately to avoid future charges.

3. Overage Credit Pack Refunds

Customers on any tier may purchase on-demand overage credit packs (1,000 additional PDF renders for $5 USD). Overage credit packs are eligible for a 100% refund within fourteen (14) calendar days of purchase provided that zero (0) PDFs have been rendered against that credit pack.

Once any portion of an overage pack has been consumed to render documents, the overage pack is non-refundable.

4. How to Cancel Your Subscription (Self-Serve)

You may cancel your recurring RenderPDF subscription at any time without fees, penalties, or long-term commitments. We offer direct, self-serve cancellation through your developer dashboard:

  1. Sign in to your RenderPDF account at https://renderpdf.vberkoz.com/app/.
  2. Navigate to the Billing section in the sidebar, or open /app/?view=billing directly.
  3. Click the "Manage billing" button.
  4. A secure tab opens to the Paddle Customer Portal (managed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record).
  5. Under your active RenderPDF subscription, click "Cancel Subscription" and confirm your choice.

You will receive an immediate email confirmation from Paddle verifying that your subscription has been cancelled and that no further charges will occur.

Alternatively, you may request cancellation by emailing our support team at support@renderpdf.vberkoz.com from your registered Google account email. We will process your cancellation within 24 hours.

5. Access and Quota After Cancellation

When you cancel a subscription, your paid access does not end immediately:

  • Retain Full Access: You retain full access to your plan's features, remaining monthly PDF quota, and existing API keys until the official end date of your current prepaid billing period.
  • No Surprise Renewals: Your payment method will not be charged again upon the conclusion of the billing period.
  • Graceful Downgrade: At the conclusion of your billing period, your account gracefully transitions to the Free tier (25 PDFs per month). Your existing API keys and stored templates are preserved.

6. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, please contact our support team:

  • Email: Send an email to support@renderpdf.vberkoz.com.
  • Subject Line: "Refund Request - [Your Account Email]".
  • Required Information: Include your registered account email, your Paddle order or transaction ID (found on your payment receipt), and a brief description of why the service did not meet your needs.

Our support team reviews and approves eligible requests within one to two (1–2) business days. Once approved, Paddle processes the refund directly to your original payment method. Funds typically appear on your credit card or bank statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your financial institution.
